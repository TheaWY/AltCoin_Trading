pump_cnn_paper (153, 9/26..10/18), f2b_shadow_paper (102), forward Upbit books 10/6..10/30 (research/hindsight_20261101.md),
counterfactual price paths from prices_1m (10/2 onward only). Figures are paper, in USDT or %.

## Counterfactuals (same entries, different exits or sides)

- F2 pump longs, price path after entry (23 trades with 1m data): +10.9% max favorable on average, but -15% at 24h and
  -19.5% at 48h. Held 4h: -2.2%. Reversed (short the pump, hold 24h): +15% per trade on this small sample.
- F2 paper test with n=153 (9/26..10/18): shorts mean +1.2%, median +2.7%, 60% win; longs mean +1.6%, median -0.3%, 47% win.
  Long mean is three trades (ex-top3 the long mean is negative). By pump size: shorts work on the two biggest quartiles
  (+5%, +4%), lose on small pumps; longs only on the biggest quartile (+11%, 20 trades) and negative on the other three.
- F2b shadow (n=102): short +2% base/trail; long 0% base, +4% with trailing exit (median +1%).
- pairs_statarb: the only profitable exit was z_revert (+27 on 5 trades). Stops took -103 on 18. The spread trades
  that hit the stop never reverted inside the window, so a wider stop would not have saved them; the entries were wrong.
- core_btc: BTC hold on the Upbit side was -10% over the same window. No version of "long BTC" made money in October.

## Upbit daily books (10/6..10/30), from the hindsight study

All 32 coins negative buy-and-hold (best BTC -9.3%, median -25.7%). All 100 pre-registered strategies negative or flat.
Ex-post max-Sharpe static long-only portfolio = 100% cash. F19 -7.5%, F18b -6.2%, F17 -9.0%, EW30 -25.5%.
Gap to cash is 100% exposure, and no ex-ante signal timed the market (all |t| < 1 on 2024-26).

## What would have made money, honestly

1. Shorting pumps after the +1h spike, not buying them. Both the main book (-55 on pump longs) and the two paper tests
   (short median +2.7%, trail-short +2%) say the same thing. Size filter: only pumps in the top half by 1h return.
2. Not running pairs_statarb at all. -174 across live + archive, 11% win on shorts. No exit rule fixes it.
3. Not holding BTC beta (core_btc) in a down month, which is the same finding as the Upbit side: October rewarded cash.
4. signal_xs shorts (69% win) deserved the capital that pairs_statarb and core_btc had. At 6 USDT a trade it could not matter.

## What this does NOT say

- 5 weeks is one regime (down + chop). Pump-short and "be in cash" both look best in a falling market by construction.
- The F2 short edge is median +2.7% with 60% win on 83 trades: real-looking but small; costs and slippage on illiquid
  pump names (US, 龙虾, GTC) can eat it. The paper test uses mid prices.
- Nothing here is a live change. Candidates to pre-register: F2 short-only with size filter (top-half ret_1h) and
  trailing exit; retire pairs_statarb; cut core_btc to the F17 overlay logic instead of a static long.
